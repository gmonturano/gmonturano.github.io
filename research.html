Participation in research projects at research institutes and universities

  • 2025–ongoing. Project funded by the Ministry of University and Research through Fondazione Eni Enrico Mattei, Milan, entitled “MUR-FRES — One, alone, indivisible: the many Italies during COVID”. The project aims to analyse territorial socioeconomic inequalities in Italy and the role of COVID-19 in widening them, using granular municipal data and advanced statistical techniques, with a European-wide comparison.
  • 2025–2027. Research activity for ProduttivItalia — Centro Studi dell’Associazione Entrobordo, in collaboration with the Department of Economics of the University of Molise, within the research project “La produttività nel mondo dei servizi”. The activity concerns the analysis of productivity dynamics in the Italian service sector, with particular attention to micro, small and medium-sized enterprises, sectoral performance over 2005–2025, intra-sectoral differences, firm size, digital innovation and internationalisation. The final output consists of a report with empirical evidence, international benchmarks and policy and managerial implications.
  • 2024–2026. GRINS Project — Spoke 7: Sustainable and Inclusive Growth. Research activity within the scope of the research grant, contributing to project reports, work packages, deliverables and scientific papers.
  • 2023. Research project funded by the International Center for Tropical Agriculture (CIAT), through the University of Molise, entitled “Support Food Insecurity Early Warning Through Conflict Analysis — Phase 3”.
  • 2023. Research project funded by the International Center for Tropical Agriculture (CIAT), through the University of Molise, entitled “Attività di Innovazione Digitale”.
  • 2022–2023. Research project of the Department of Economics “Marco Biagi”, funded through university research funds 2022, entitled “The roles of instructor-student similarities, surnames, and beauty on UNIMORE students’ outcomes and early career jobs”.

Formal research appointments from institutions and organizations

  • 2026. Paid research appointment for WITHUB S.p.A., within the project entitled “La transizione ecologica nell’automotive e il ruolo della domanda pubblica: evidenze empiriche dalla pubblica amministrazione italiana (2019–2025)”. The activity concerns the statistical evaluation of vehicle registration data from Italian public administrations, with the aim of analysing the direction and speed of change in the ecological transition of the automotive sector.
  • 2026. Paid research appointment for A.P.S. Entrobordo and Mama Industry S.r.l., concerning the analysis of productivity in the service sector with reference to micro, small and medium-sized enterprises with 4–9 employees. The appointment includes data collection and processing, statistical-economic analysis and the preparation of summary reports, with both a national study and a territorial study focused on the Abruzzo Region.
