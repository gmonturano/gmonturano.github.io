I am an Assistant Professor (RTDA) at the University of Salento and a Senior Researcher at Fondazione Eni Enrico Mattei.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Department of Economics, Management and Business Law, University of Bari Aldo Moro, and a researcher at the Laboratory of Applied Economics. I obtained my PhD from the Department of Economics Marco Biagi, University of Modena and Reggio Emilia.

I was also a Visiting Research Fellow at the Solvay Brussels School of Economics and Management, Université Libre de Bruxelles (ULB).

My research focuses on public policy evaluation, regional and spatial economics, applied econometrics, transport accessibility, innovation, inequality and territorial development. I work with administrative, spatial and longitudinal data, combining econometric methods, machine learning tools and reproducible computational workflows.

I teach courses in economics, empirical methods, public policy, statistics, econometrics and data analysis. I also contribute to research projects on territorial inequalities, productivity, transport, digital infrastructure and innovation policy.

Main programming languages: R Python
