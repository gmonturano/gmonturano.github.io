Presentation

Assistant Professor (RTDA) in Political Economy, University of Lecce
Senior Researcher, Fondazione Eni Enrico Mattei

I am a quantitative economist and data scientist. I am currently an Assistant Professor (RTDA) in Political Economy at the Department of Economics of the University of Lecce and a Senior Researcher at the Fondazione Eni Enrico Mattei in Milan.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Applied Economics at the Department of Economics, Management and Business Law of the University of Bari, where I remain affiliated as a researcher at the Laboratory of Applied Economics (LEA). I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the Department of Economics of the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia. I was also a Visiting Research Fellow in Economics and Econometrics at the Solvay Brussels School of Economics and Management, Université Libre de Bruxelles (ULB).

I have teaching experience in economics and statistics at several universities and collaborate on research projects with a range of academic and policy institutions. My main scientific interests include causal inference, spatial econometrics, network analysis, and predictive estimation using machine learning algorithms for public policy evaluation.

My research focuses on public economics, regional development, cohesion policies, socioeconomic inequalities, and the labour market, with a particular emphasis on granular data at the micro-territorial and individual level. I program mainly in R and Python.
