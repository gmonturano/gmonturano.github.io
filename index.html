I am currently an Assistant Professor (RTDA) in Political Economy at the Department of Economics of the University of Salento (Unisalento) and a Senior Researcher at the Fondazione Eni Enrico Mattei (FEEM) in Milan.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Applied Economics at the Department of Economics, Management and Business Law (DEMDI) of the University of Bari, where I remain affiliated as a researcher at the Laboratory of Applied Economics (LEA). I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the Department of Economics (DEMB) of the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia. I was also a Visiting Research Fellow at the Solvay Brussels School of Economics and Management, Université Libre de Bruxelles (ULB).

I have teaching experience in economics and statistics at several universities and collaborate on research projects with a range of academic and policy institutions.

My research focuses on public economics, regional development, cohesion policies, socioeconomic inequalities, and the labour market, with a particular emphasis on granular data at the micro-territorial and individual level. My main scientific interests include causal inference, spatial econometrics, network analysis, and predictive estimation using machine learning algorithms, text analysis, and web scraping for public policy evaluation.
