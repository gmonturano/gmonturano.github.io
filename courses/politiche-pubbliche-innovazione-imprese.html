Current course

Politiche Pubbliche per l’Innovazione e le Imprese

Course materials, notices, slides and teaching resources.

The course introduces the economic rationale, instruments and evaluation methods of public policies for innovation and firms. Topics include market failures, knowledge spillovers, public intervention, financing of innovation, industrial policy, territorial policy and empirical approaches to policy evaluation.

University of Salento · Department of Economics · Academic year 2025–2026 · Instructor: Gianluca Monturano

Programme

  • Public economics of innovation and the rationale for public intervention.
  • Knowledge as an economic good: non-rivalry, imperfect excludability and appropriability.
  • Externalities, knowledge spillovers and the divergence between private and social returns.
  • Public instruments for innovation: patents, subsidies, tax credits, public research and public demand.
  • Additionality, deadweight, crowding-in and crowding-out.
  • Financing constraints, information asymmetries and innovation investment.
  • Industrial policy and firm-level competitiveness.
  • Territorial policies, regional development and innovation systems.
  • Empirical methods for policy evaluation and causal inference.
  • Applications to Italian and European public policies.
