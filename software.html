Fuel Costs and Spatial Rail Accessibility

Network-Based 3SFCA · Spatial Econometrics · Rail Accessibility

Fuel Costs and Spatial Rail Accessibility: Econometric Evidence from an Empirically-Weighted Network-Based 3SFCA Model

Published Article R TWFE IV / 2SLS DML Spatial Accessibility

The paper develops an empirically weighted, network-based Three-Step Floating Catchment Area approach to investigate spatial rail accessibility and fuel costs. The replication package contains the main TWFE, IV/2SLS and Double Machine Learning estimation scripts, a metadata workbook and supporting panel-data material.

Spatial Consequences of Tax Reforms

Tax Compliance · Difference-in-Differences · Spatial Heterogeneity

Spatial consequences of tax reforms on tax compliance: econometric evidence from the Italian Minimum Taxpayer Regime

Published Article R Continuous DiD TWFE Tax Compliance Restricted Data

The study investigates how the Italian Minimum Taxpayer Regime affects tax compliance across provinces, considering heterogeneous policy exposure, self-employment and territorial characteristics. The repository provides selected estimation code and simulated data for methodological inspection. Confidential tax data are not publicly distributed.

Digital Divide and Income Inequality

Broadband Infrastructure · Inequality · Causal Inference

Digital divide and income inequality: causal evidence from Italian provinces

Published Article R DiD Broadband Income Inequality Panel Data

The paper evaluates the relationship between broadband infrastructure expansion and income inequality across Italian provinces. The empirical framework investigates continuous treatment intensity, provincial heterogeneity and alternative measures of inequality, using panel-data methods and causal identification strategies.
